A small ritual. A different perspective.

Same path.
New things to notice.

A listening walk starts with attention. Technology can wait until you get home.

01

BEFORE YOU GO

Choose the ordinary.

The corner, the trees, the way home. Pick three familiar stops. There is no perfect route to find.

Make a pocket journal ↗
02

OUT IN THE WORLD

Listen for a minute.

Near or far. Continuous or passing. Keep a few words before looking for labels. A note is enough.

No streaks. No score. No GPS.
03

WHEN YOU RETURN

Keep your perspective.

Revisit your notes. In the desktop journal, local AI offers sound candidates from recordings. Replay, question, correct.
