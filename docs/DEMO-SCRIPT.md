# Soundwalk Atlas demo — recording script

Prepared October 9, 2026. Target: a clear 2–3 minute video, with readable controls and genuine app responses. Record the final reviewed build; do not splice prerecorded predictions into an inference action. The public demo can use the explicitly synthetic sample without inventing an outdoor trial.

## Preparation

- Run the setup diagnostic, launch the app and prepare the model before recording. State that the initial download happened earlier.
- Use an isolated demo journal. Do not display private notes, personal files, precise locations, account tokens or the user's WhatsApp recordings.
- Use 1280 × 800 or larger, normal zoom and a quiet desktop. Keep sound understandable and include captions. Use reduced motion if recording causes discomfort; animations are optional presentation.
- Load the labelled synthetic example. Confirm the two sample visits and their recordings are available. Never rename a synthetic fixture as a real place recording.
- Rehearse the actual controls once. Keep any real inference wait in the video or clearly label a time cut. Do not show synthetic preloaded results as newly computed inference.

## Shot list and narration

| Time | Screen action | Suggested narration |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:15 | Landing view, gentle scroll into journal | “Soundwalk Atlas is a listening journal. Prepare a few stops, leave the screen behind, and bring your observations home.” |
| 0:15–0:35 | Open preparation/listening card; show stable stops and print option | “The outdoor part uses a printed card and your normal phone recorder. You do not need to keep this browser open on the walk.” |
| 0:35–0:55 | Focus mode; save a clearly labelled demonstration note without audio | “Notes work on their own. Your observation comes first; recording is optional.” |
| 0:55–1:20 | Import a permitted synthetic recording; show actual waveform and selected interval | “Back on the laptop, choose the part you want to review. This waveform is decoded from the file, and the previous analysed interval remains visible.” |
| 1:20–1:45 | Start actual analysis, show provisional candidates and technical detail | “CLAP runs locally after its one-time download. These are relative matches to eleven prompts, not confidence percentages or proof of a sound.” |
| 1:45–2:05 | Replay; select “none fits” for a procedural fixture or a justified human label, save | “The person decides. You can reject every candidate, add your own label, or preserve uncertainty. Suggestions do not fill in your answer.” |
| 2:05–2:30 | Compare the two synthetic visits; show notes and review status | “Returning to the same stops gives you a personal comparison. These samples demonstrate the workflow, not an environmental trend.” |
| 2:30–2:50 | Backup/restore controls and model provenance; finish on journal | “Original recordings and reviews can stay in a private portable backup. The source, model revision and review evidence are open.” |

Opening or closing on-screen caption: **Synthetic demonstration — no outdoor trial or independent accuracy claim.**

If genuine fieldwork is later included, replace only the corresponding shots and narration with the actual date, coarse context, permissions and observed outcome. Preserve missing recordings and mistaken predictions. Do not claim zero screen interaction or improved wellbeing without measuring it.
